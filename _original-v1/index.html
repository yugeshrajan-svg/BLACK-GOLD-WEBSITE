06 / A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What types of oil can I sell?+

Black Gold collects used engine oil, hydraulic oil and transfer oil. Specify the oil type when preparing your pickup.

Who can register as a seller?+

Registered businesses, including car service centres, bike service centres and industrial units. Your GST number and address proof are verified before the first pickup.

How is the selling price decided?+

Black Gold sets a price per litre for each oil type. Check the current rate in the seller app before selling. This website does not display live rates.

How is my oil measured?+

The collection team measures the oil at your premises, in front of you. A digital receipt records your collection.

Where do you collect?+

Black Gold serves businesses in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Confirm availability for your exact locality before arranging a pickup.

Is there a minimum pickup quantity?+

The minimum quantity and any collection charges need to be confirmed with Black Gold before booking. You can prepare an enquiry with your oil type and estimated volume below.
