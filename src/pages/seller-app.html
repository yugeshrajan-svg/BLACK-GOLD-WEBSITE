Collection & payment FAQs →
Can I use the app in Tamil?

Yes. Choose English or Tamil from Profile to change the language throughout the Seller App.

How do I change or cancel a listing?

After submitting, contact the Black Gold office to request a change or cancellation. Submitted listings cannot be edited or cancelled inside the app.

What if I forget my password?

Contact the Black Gold office for a temporary password. After signing in, change it from Profile.

Can I delete my account?

You can request account deletion from Profile. Your phone number, address, shop location and documents are removed; completed sale records needed for tax are retained.
