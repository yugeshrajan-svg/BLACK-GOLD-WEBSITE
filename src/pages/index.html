Where do you collect?

We serve businesses in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Confirm availability for your exact locality before arranging a pickup.

How is the price set?

Each oil type has a price per litre. Check the current rate in the seller app and confirm it with Black Gold. This website does not show live rates.

Is there a minimum quantity?

Confirm the minimum quantity and any collection charges with Black Gold. Include your oil type and approximate volume in your enquiry.

Does this website book my pickup?

The form creates an enquiry summary. Download it, copy it, or choose to share it on WhatsApp. Nothing is sent automatically, and a pickup is only confirmed separately with Black Gold.
