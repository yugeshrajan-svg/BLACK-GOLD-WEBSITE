13 questions

What oils can I sell?

Black Gold collects used and waste oil only, in three types:

  • Used and waste engine oil
  • Used and waste hydraulic oil
  • Used and waste transformer oil

Factories and industrial units can also enquire about waste cutting oil, used coolant, cleaners, and oily washing water or sludge on the For industry page.

Who can register?
  • Registered businesses only
  • Car & bike service centres, industrial units
  • GST and address proof verified first
Can a fleet or depot sell used oil?

Please confirm fleet or depot eligibility with Black Gold before registering. Share your business type, location, oil type and approximate volume by emailing {{contactEmail}}. The website does not confirm fleet eligibility or book a collection.

How is the price set?
  • A price per litre for each oil type
  • Current rate shown in the seller app
  • No live rates on this website
How is my oil measured?

The driver measures the oil at your premises, checks its quality and records a photo. The collected quantity is recorded on your receipt.

Where do you collect?
  • Businesses in Chennai, Tamil Nadu
  • Confirm your locality before booking
Is there a minimum quantity?

The minimum pickup is 50 litres. Smaller workshops can keep their oil ready until they reach that quantity. For barrels, confirm the total volume and any collection charges with Black Gold.

Does this website book my pickup?

The website creates an enquiry summary that you can download, copy, or send yourself on WhatsApp or by email to {{contactEmail}}. Nothing is sent automatically. A pickup is confirmed separately with Black Gold.

When should I share my pickup code?

Give the driver your four-digit code only after your oil has been measured and loaded. The code completes the pickup. Never share it over a phone call.

How do I receive my payment?

Payment is made at pickup by UPI to the ID in your app Profile, or in cash. Your receipt records the payment and GST details.

What if the measured quantity is different?

The driver records the measured quantity with a photo. Any difference from your listing is flagged to the office, and payment is based on the quantity collected.

Can I change or cancel a submitted listing?

Email {{contactEmail}} for help with a submitted listing. Changes and cancellations are handled by the office rather than inside the app.

How do I contact Black Gold support?

Email {{contactEmail}} for enquiries and support, or call {{contactDisplay}}. Include your business name and, for an existing order, your listing details.
