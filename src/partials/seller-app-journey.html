Scroll the story or choose a step

  1. 01You · Get started

    Register your business.

    Open Black Gold and add your business name, type, phone number and GST number. Save your shop location so the driver can find you.

    What to have ready ↓
  2. 02You + office · Get verified

    A quick proof check.

    Upload a photo of an electricity bill, rental agreement or property tax receipt. The office checks your business details before your first pickup.

    One setup for your future collections
  3. 03You · Start a sale

    List the oil you have.

    Choose engine, hydraulic or transformer oil and enter your quantity. Review the current rate and estimated amount before GST, then submit your listing.

    At least 50 litres ready for pickup
  4. 04Office · Confirm the plan

    Your pickup is planned.

    Black Gold confirms the price and quantity and arranges collection. Follow the confirmation in Orders and Alerts.

    Need to change a listing? Contact the office.
  5. 05You · Follow the vehicle

    See your pickup coming.

    Check the driver’s name and live vehicle map in your order. Updates arrive automatically, and your four-digit pickup code appears in the app.

    Keep your code until the oil is measured and loaded
  6. 06Driver + you · At your shop

    Every litre is recorded.

    The driver measures your oil in front of you, checks its quality and records a photo. Any difference from your listing is flagged to the office.

    Payment uses the quantity actually collected
  7. 07You · Authorise completion

    Loaded? Now share the code.

    Once your oil is measured and loaded, give the driver your four-digit pickup code. The driver needs it to complete collection.

    Never share the code over a phone call
  8. 08Black Gold · Sale complete

    Get paid. Keep the receipt.

    Receive payment by UPI or cash for the oil collected. Your completed order holds the quantity, agreed rate, payment and GST details in a receipt you can print or share.
